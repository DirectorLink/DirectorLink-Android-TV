## DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications 1.0.0-beta.2

**Beta.** The driver now works with one TV app, **PiPup — TV Notifications** by Alexander Savin (free on Google Play), set up as PiPup's own documentation describes it, and sends the picture **inside** the pop-up. Tested on a Sony XR-77A95L (Google TV) with PiPup 0.3.4.

Free, open-source Control4 driver for TVs with Google TV or Android TV. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers). Free. No subscription, no license key, no account with us. Works on its own: DirectorLink is not required.

### Why beta.1 showed nothing

Beta.1 spoke to two other TV apps: "Notifications for Android TV" (its default, port 7676) and a different fork of PiPup. On a TV with Alexander Savin's PiPup nothing answered on port 7676, so the test notification failed. Beta.2 speaks to that PiPup only, the way it documents: `/status` before each pop-up, then `/notify`.

### Tested on

A Sony XR-77A95L (Google TV) with PiPup 0.3.4, from a computer on the TV's network running the driver's own code: Test Notification, a DirectorLink doorbell notification in Hebrew, Show Notification, a picture carried inline and a picture by address, all shown. Not yet confirmed from a Control4 controller.

### What changed

- **One app: PiPup — TV Notifications** (Google Play: `nyc.savin.pipup`). The **TV App** property is gone. Other apps called PiPup take other requests and do not work with the driver.
- **PiPup's API as it documents it:** `GET /status` before each pop-up (a screen that is off gets nothing), `POST /notify` with JSON, `GET /ping` for Find TVs.
- **New properties:** **PiPup Port** (7979) and **PiPup Token** (only when a token is set in PiPup; sent as `Authorization: Bearer`, never logged). **Status** says when PiPup wants a token, or when its IP allowlist leaves the controller out.
- **The picture inside the pop-up:** the driver fetches the notification's picture itself (on the controller, so `127.0.0.1` works, and a login in a camera's address is sent as a login) and sends it inline. The TV never has to reach the picture's address. A picture not fetched within 2 seconds, or larger than 1.5 MB, goes as its address.
- **Find TVs** marks the TVs where PiPup answers.
- No more binary data in requests: the icon the other app needed is gone.

### Installing

1. On the TV, install **PiPup — TV Notifications** from Google Play, open it once, select **Grant display permission**, and press its test button.
2. Upload the driver with **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**.
3. Search **DirectorLink**, add **DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications** to the TV's room, and pick the TV in **TVs Found** (marked *PiPup answers*) or type its IP address.
4. Run **Test Notification**.

Requirements: Control4 OS 3.3.0 or newer, Composer Pro, a TV with Google TV or Android TV (Android 7 or newer). Full steps and the list of TVs: the README.

### Updating

- **From 1.0.0-beta.1:** **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent** with this file, then install PiPup on the TV (above). The TV App property is gone; the other settings and the programming stay.

### Known issues

- **Not yet confirmed from a Control4 controller** (the reason it is still a beta).
- **Find TVs**, from a Control4 driver, is not yet confirmed on a real controller: type the IP address when it finds nothing (PiPup's screen shows it).
- **Fire TV** has no Google Play: PiPup's APK can be installed by hand there, not tested.
