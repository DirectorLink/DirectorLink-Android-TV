## DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications 1.0.0

First full release. Put the free **PiPup — TV Notifications** app on a TV with Google TV or Android TV, add the driver, and DirectorLink's notifications pop up on the TV **with a picture**: who is at the door or the gate. Composer programming can show its own, on one TV or on all of them.

Free, open-source Control4 driver for TVs with Google TV or Android TV. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers). Free. No subscription, no license key, no account with us. Works on its own: DirectorLink is not required.

### Tested on

A Sony XR-77A95L (Google TV) with PiPup 0.3.4, from a Control4 controller: Find TVs found it (*PiPup answers*), Test Notification and Show Notification with a picture (fetched by the controller and sent inside the pop-up) showed on it. From a computer on the TV's network, running the driver's own code: pop-ups in English and Hebrew, pictures inline and by address.

### What it does

- **On the home network only**, with **PiPup — TV Notifications** by Alexander Savin (free on Google Play) on the TV. No cloud, no account.
- **The picture inside the pop-up**: DirectorLink's picture with a ring, or a camera's snapshot from programming, fetched by the controller (a login in a camera's address is sent as a login), so the TV never has to reach it.
- **Only when the screen is on**: the driver asks PiPup first. Off, or no answer within 3 seconds, the pop-up is dropped with one log line. It never turns the TV on.
- **One copy per TV**, each with its own choice of kinds (Doorbell, Camera Alerts, Doors and Gates, Alarm, Other), Quiet Hours, and where and how long the pop-up shows.
- **Show Notification** (Title, Message, Picture URL, **Send To**): *Send To All TVs* reaches every TV's copy with one command.
- **One pop-up every 5 seconds at most**, a doorbell or the alarm first; text as sent, Hebrew included.
- **Find TVs** lists the TVs on the network, marking the ones where PiPup answers.
- **PiPup Token** and **PiPup Port** when PiPup's settings ask for them; Status says when PiPup wants a token or its IP allowlist leaves the controller out.
- **With DirectorLink 1.13**: the DirectorLink notification agreement, version 1, the same as DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications.

### Installing

1. On the TV, install **PiPup — TV Notifications** from Google Play, open it once, select **Grant display permission**, and press its test button.
2. Upload the driver with **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**.
3. Search **DirectorLink**, add **DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications** to the TV's room (once per TV), and pick the TV in **TVs Found** (marked *PiPup answers*) or type its IP address.
4. Run **Test Notification**.

Requirements: Control4 OS 3.3.0 or newer, Composer Pro, a TV with Google TV or Android TV (Android 7 or newer). Full steps and the list of TVs: [directorlink.io/drivers/android-tv](https://directorlink.io/drivers/android-tv).

### Updating

- **From 1.0.0-beta.3:** the list's first TV becomes **TV IP Address**; the log names the other TVs of the list: add a copy of the driver for each.
- **From 1.0.0-beta.2:** settings and programming stay.
- **From 1.0.0-beta.1:** install PiPup on the TV; the TV App property is gone.

### Known issues

- **Only PiPup — TV Notifications** by Alexander Savin: other apps called PiPup take other requests and do not work with the driver.
- **Send To All TVs** reaches the other copies with `C4:SendToDevice`; tested against the fake Director, not yet with several TVs on a real controller.
- **Fire TV** has no Google Play: PiPup's APK can be installed by hand there, not tested.
