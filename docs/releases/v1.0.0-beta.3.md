## DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications 1.0.0-beta.3

**Beta.** One driver for **several TVs**: list them in **TV IP Addresses**, and every notification, with its picture, shows on all of them at once.

Free, open-source Control4 driver for TVs with Google TV or Android TV, with the free **PiPup — TV Notifications** app by Alexander Savin on each TV. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers). Free. No subscription, no license key, no account with us. Works on its own: DirectorLink is not required.

### Tested on

- **From a Control4 controller** (1.0.0-beta.2, the same way of talking to PiPup): a Sony XR-77A95L (Google TV) with PiPup 0.3.4. Find TVs found it (*PiPup answers*); Test Notification and a pop-up with a picture showed (the picture fetched by the controller and sent inside the pop-up).
- **Several TVs** (this version), from a computer on the same network running the driver's own code: a list of two, the Sony and a box without PiPup. Shown on the Sony; the box named in the log and in **Status** (*PiPup not answering*).

### What changed

- **TV IP Addresses**: the TVs, separated by commas (`192.168.1.50, 192.168.1.51`, or `ip:port` for a TV whose PiPup uses another port), up to 16. It replaces **TV IP Address**; from beta.1 or beta.2 the driver moves the TV's address into it by itself.
- **TVs**: each TV, its state and PiPup's version. It replaces **TV**.
- **Every TV at once**: the picture is fetched once and shown on every TV whose screen is on. *Notification Shown* when at least one TV showed it, *Notification Failed* when none did. The log says on which TVs, and why not on the others.
- **Each TV is checked on its own**; **Status** says *Ready: 2 TVs*, or which TVs are not ready and why.
- **TVs Found**: picking a TV adds it to the list.
- TVs that need other settings (Quiet Hours for a child's room) get a second copy of the driver.

### Installing

1. On each TV, install **PiPup — TV Notifications** from Google Play, open it once, select **Grant display permission**, and press its test button.
2. Upload the driver with **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**.
3. Search **DirectorLink**, add **DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications** once, and pick each TV in **TVs Found** (marked *PiPup answers*), or type the addresses in **TV IP Addresses**.
4. Run **Test Notification**.

Requirements: Control4 OS 3.3.0 or newer, Composer Pro, TVs with Google TV or Android TV (Android 7 or newer). Full steps and the list of TVs: the README.

### Updating

- **From 1.0.0-beta.2 or beta.1:** **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent** with this file. The TV's address moves to **TV IP Addresses**; the other settings and the programming stay. From beta.1, install PiPup on the TV.

### Known issues

- **Several TVs from a Control4 controller** not yet confirmed (one TV was).
- **Fire TV** has no Google Play: PiPup's APK can be installed by hand there, not tested.
