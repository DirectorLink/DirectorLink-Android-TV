DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications BETA

Free, open-source Control4 driver: notifications as a pop-up with a picture on TVs with Google TV, Android TV or Fire TV. Part of DirectorLink Drivers.

https://directorlink.io/drivers/android-tv

A doorbell ring with who is at the door, a camera alert, a door or gate opened, the alarm: on the TV, over whatever is on. On the home network only, with a free app on the TV.

Which TVs

In the TV's Settings → About: Google TV, Android TV or Fire OS 7 / 8 works (Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense Google TV, Sharp, Xiaomi, Philips 2021-2025, Panasonic Google TV and Fire TV models, Fire TV TVs and sticks, Google TV Streamer, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield...). Samsung, LG (use DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications), Vizio, Roku TVs, VIDAA and Titan OS TVs, and the newest Fire TV sticks with Vega OS do not.

Setup

  1. Set up the app on the TV (above), give the TV a fixed IP address in the router, and turn it on.
  2. Choose the TV App.
  3. Pick the TV in TVs Found (the driver looks for TVs when it is added, the ones where the app answers first; Find TVs looks again), or type its address in TV IP Address. Status says Ready.
  4. Run Test Notification.
  5. Choose what this TV shows (Doorbell, Camera Alerts, Doors and Gates, Alarm, Other), Quiet Hours, and the look (Position, Seconds On Screen, Pictures).

When a pop-up shows

The TV has 3 seconds to take the connection; off or not reachable, the pop-up is dropped with one line in the log, and Notification Failed fires. With PiPup, a screen that is off gets nothing. The driver never turns the TV on. One pop-up every 5 seconds at most on each TV, a doorbell or the alarm first. Only the kinds switched on, nothing during Quiet Hours (Show Notification ignores the kinds; Test Notification always shows). The text is shown as sent, Hebrew included.

The picture is fetched by the TV from the address the notification gives: DirectorLink's PICTURE (on the controller; 127.0.0.1 becomes the controller's address), or Show Notification's Picture URL, for example a camera's snapshot address that opens without a login prompt. The TV must reach it on the home network. A login in an address is never written to the log.

Checks: every minute while the app answers; when it does not, after 5 s, 30 s, 2 min, then every 5 min.

Programming reference

Command: SHOW_NOTIFICATION (Title, Message, Picture URL): a pop-up, whatever the kinds, not during Quiet Hours. Events: Notification Shown · Notification Failed (off, not reachable, the app not running, or no answer). Variable: LAST_NOTIFICATION (STRING), the text of the last pop-up shown or failed, set before the event fires.

DirectorLink

DirectorLink 1.13 and newer sends its notifications to this driver as soon as both are in the project (DirectorLink is not required). The driver follows the DirectorLink notification agreement, version 1, the same as DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications:

WhatThe driver
MarkerString variable DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY = 1, set at every start, after the driver's other variables (never reordering them).
KindsString variable DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS: the kinds switched on, comma-separated, from doorbell, camera, door, alarm, other; updated whenever a property changes.
A notificationThe command DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY by C4:SendToDevice, taken in ExecuteCommand and ReceivedFromProxy, with VERSION (1), ID (unique; a repeat within 10 minutes is ignored), KIND, TITLE and MESSAGE (final text in the home's language, shown exactly as given), PICTURE (optional, an http address on the controller with a JPEG for 60 seconds; the TV fetches it, so it must answer on the controller's home-network address too).
RulesNever block. Only the kinds switched on, nothing during Quiet Hours. At most one pop-up every 5 seconds per TV, a doorbell or the alarm first. Set LAST_NOTIFICATION, then fire Notification Shown or Notification Failed.

The variables come in this order: LAST_NOTIFICATION, DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY, DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS.
