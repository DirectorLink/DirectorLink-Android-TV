DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications BETA

Free, open-source Control4 driver: notifications as a pop-up with a picture on TVs with Google TV or Android TV, with PiPup. Part of DirectorLink Drivers.

https://directorlink.io/drivers/android-tv

A doorbell ring with who is at the door, a camera alert, a door or gate opened, the alarm: on the TV, over whatever is on. On the home network only, with the free PiPup app on the TV.

The app on the TV: PiPup

The TV runs PiPup — TV Notifications by Alexander Savin, free on Google Play (nyc.savin.pipup, pipup.android.savin.nyc). Only this PiPup: other apps called PiPup take other requests and do not work with the driver.

  1. On the TV, install PiPup — TV Notifications from Google Play.
  2. Open PiPup once and select Grant display permission (Display over other apps; on some older Android TV models it is granted from a computer with adb, see PiPup's instructions).
  3. PiPup's screen shows the TV's address and a test button: press it, a pop-up shows.
  4. Leave PiPup's token and IP allowlist off, or enter the token in PiPup Token and add the controller's address to the allowlist.

Which TVs

A TV or box with Google TV or Android TV (Android 7 or newer) and Google Play: Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense Google TV, Sharp, Xiaomi, Philips 2021-2025, Panasonic Google TV models, Google TV Streamer, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield... Samsung, LG (use DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications), Vizio, Roku TVs, VIDAA and Titan OS TVs do not. Fire TV has no Google Play (PiPup's APK by hand, not tested).

Setup

  1. Set up PiPup on the TV (above), give the TV a fixed IP address in the router, and turn it on.
  2. Pick the TV in TVs Found (the driver looks for TVs when it is added; PiPup answers marks the TVs with PiPup; Find TVs looks again), or type its address in TV IP Address. Status says Ready.
  3. Run Test Notification.
  4. Choose what this TV shows (Doorbell, Camera Alerts, Doors and Gates, Alarm, Other), Quiet Hours, and the look (Position, Seconds On Screen, Pictures).

When a pop-up shows

Only when the screen is on: just before each pop-up the driver asks PiPup (/status). The TV has 3 seconds to answer; off or not reachable, the pop-up is dropped with one line in the log, and Notification Failed fires. The driver never turns the TV on. One pop-up every 5 seconds at most on each TV, a doorbell or the alarm first; a new pop-up replaces the one still showing. Only the kinds switched on, nothing during Quiet Hours (Show Notification ignores the kinds; Test Notification always shows). The text is shown as sent, Hebrew included.

The picture: the driver fetches DirectorLink's PICTURE (on the controller; 127.0.0.1 works) or Show Notification's Picture URL (for example a camera's snapshot; a login written in the address, http://user:password@..., is sent as a login) and sends it inside the pop-up, so the TV never has to reach it. Not fetched within 2 seconds, or larger than 1.5 MB: the address goes instead, for PiPup to fetch. A token or a login is never written to the log.

Checks: every minute while PiPup answers; when it does not, after 5 s, 30 s, 2 min, then every 5 min.

Programming reference

Command: SHOW_NOTIFICATION (Title, Message, Picture URL): a pop-up, whatever the kinds, not during Quiet Hours. Events: Notification Shown · Notification Failed (off, not reachable, PiPup not running or refusing, or no answer). Variable: LAST_NOTIFICATION (STRING), the text of the last pop-up shown or failed, set before the event fires.

DirectorLink

DirectorLink 1.13 and newer sends its notifications to this driver as soon as both are in the project (DirectorLink is not required). The driver follows the DirectorLink notification agreement, version 1, the same as DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications:

WhatThe driver
MarkerString variable DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY = 1, set at every start, after the driver's other variables (never reordering them).
KindsString variable DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS: the kinds switched on, comma-separated, from doorbell, camera, door, alarm, other; updated whenever a property changes.
A notificationThe command DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY by C4:SendToDevice, taken in ExecuteCommand and ReceivedFromProxy, with VERSION (1), ID (unique; a repeat within 10 minutes is ignored), KIND, TITLE and MESSAGE (final text in the home's language, shown exactly as given), PICTURE (optional, an http address on the controller with a JPEG for 60 seconds; the driver fetches it and shows it in the pop-up).
RulesNever block. Only the kinds switched on, nothing during Quiet Hours. At most one pop-up every 5 seconds per TV, a doorbell or the alarm first. Set LAST_NOTIFICATION, then fire Notification Shown or Notification Failed.

The variables come in this order: LAST_NOTIFICATION, DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY, DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS.
